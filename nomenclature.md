# Squelch: Nomenclature

**REACTION:** Selects Radiation, Fission, Sludge, Chemical or Alien behavior.
**MODE, GRID:** Trigger scheduling mode and grid division.
**ENRICHMENT, FLUX, PROBABILITY:** Input conditioning, detector level and trigger chance.
**REACTIVITY, VOLATILITY, HALFLIFE, DECAY:** Reaction response and envelope timing.
**SPREAD, TOXICITY, CONTAINMENT, DRIVE:** Grain placement, coloration, feedback and excitation.
**CONTAMINATION, EXPOSURE, COLLIMATOR, FALLOUT, AFTERGLOW:** Reaction modulation and output character.
**IONIZE, IONIZEAMOUNT, MELTDOWN:** Haze and high-energy mode controls.
**SEED, CLIP, MIX:** Determinism, clipping mode and wet/dry mix.

Parameter IDs, labels and ranges are in `Source/Parameters.h`. Names not present in that table are not exposed controls.
